Privacy

Scanning, QR creation and PDF export all happen on your device. Nothing is uploaded. History is kept in this app’s browser storage, which Android or you can clear at any time. PDF files themselves are saved only where you download them.

Camera help

If the camera is blocked, open Android Settings → Apps → Scan & Share → Permissions → Camera. Camera access needs HTTPS or an app that grants it.

Help

Scan opens the live camera or reads a code from an image. Documents and Images to PDF share one editor: add pages, adjust, reorder, then create the PDF. There is no automatic edge detection or OCR.
